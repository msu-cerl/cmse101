# AI and Society Course

Welcome to the AI and Society course! This site contains all course materials including readings, assignments, and the syllabus.

## Course Overview

This is a hybrid/hyflex course exploring the intersection of artificial intelligence and society. We examine AI's impact on ethics, policy, bias, employment, and more.

**Course Format:** Readings and materials are available here as an open e-book. All content is also integrated with our D2L Brightspace system.
