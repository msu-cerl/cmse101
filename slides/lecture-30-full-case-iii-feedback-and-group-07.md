# What Stood Out

# Small Things to Bring Into Your Essay

- **Every (Author, year) in the text needs a reference entry.** This was the most common gap.
- **Cite the primary document** (the order, the filing, the judgment) when you have it, not a summary.
- **Keep numbers consistent** across sections. If sources disagree, say who said what.
- **Write disagreements about the case**, not about the process.

*Your Case Design essay is graded on the same habits.*
