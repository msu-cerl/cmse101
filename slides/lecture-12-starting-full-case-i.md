# From Frameworks to Your Own Case

For four weeks we built DTPA one piece at a time: **Data** (Week 1), **Tools** (Week 2), **Practices** (Week 3), **Actions** (Week 4).

Starting today, you use all four at once, and **you find the case.**

- Weeks 1–4 asked: *where have we been?* — foundations and mechanics.
- Weeks 5–10 ask: *where are we now?* — real deployments, domain by domain.
- Three rounds, two weeks each. Today starts **Full Case I.**

---

# Full Case I: Choose a Domain

Your group picks one broad domain, then narrows to **one specific, documented case** inside it.

- 🏥 **Healthcare**
- 🎨 **Art & Creative Industries**
- 🎓 **Education**
- 🏟️ **Athletics & Sports**
- 🏛️ **Politics & Government**

**Domain is not a case.** *"AI in healthcare"* is not a case; *a specific hospital's use of a named documentation tool* is.

Your case needs: a **named tool**, a **named organization**, and a **documented deployment** (something that happened, with a date, from a citable source).

# Your Case Study Document

Same template family as your weekly Case Studies, split into two parts:

- **Part A (Week 5 Update)** — a checkpoint, not a draft. Sections 0–8 walk you through: your case statement, how you got there, sources so far, DTPA readiness, your thinnest part, disagreements, and your plan for Week 6.
- **Part B (Full Case)** — the complete DTPA analysis and Four Pillars reflection, same standard as your weekly Case Studies, plus **Similar Cases** and a group reflection.

**Use Section 0** to dump links, notes, and half-finished thoughts as you go — nothing there is graded, but nothing gets lost either.

# The Trap to Avoid

**Do not stop at the first case you find, and do not default to the domains materials we posted.**

Those readings are the most visible stories in each domain — that's exactly why they're a place to **start**, not a place to **finish**.

- Follow one source to another.
- The best cases so far have come from groups chasing something nobody handed them.
- 🔎 [AI Incident Database](https://incidentdatabase.ai/) · [AI Hallucination Cases Database](https://www.damiencharlotin.com/hallucinations/) · [404 Media](https://www.404media.co/)

# New: The Honors Option

An independent research option on the **energy, water, and political costs of AI computing.**

- **Eligibility:** 3.0+ in CMSE 101 — this builds on the course, it doesn't replace it.
- **Individual** project.
- Framed by **critical pragmatism**: name what's exploitative or oversold about a system, *and* end with something usable — a policy recommendation, a design constraint, practical guidance someone could reasonably argue against.

Every topic must address two required lenses:

1. **Hype vs. evidence** — hold an industry/marketing claim up against independent evidence.
2. **Labor and exploitation** — name who makes the system possible, and who's exposed to its harms.
