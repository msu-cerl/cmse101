# The Case Design Project

---

# From Analyzing to Proposing

Your Case Studies **analyze** something that already happened. Case Design **proposes** something that should happen.

- Same DTPA framework but we reverse the direction.
- **One document that grows** over the whole semester: Scaffold 1 → Scaffold 2 → Final Case Design.
- Each version gets instructor feedback, and the next version must respond to it in writing.
- Teams of **3–5**, formed this week. *Can be your Case Studies group.*

Your Case Studies are your best preparation. Many strong Case Designs start from a case your group already analyzed.

---

# Choose a Pathway

| Pathway | Goal | You must deliver |
|--------|------|------------------------|
| **I. Proactive AI Solution** | Design an AI application that addresses a societal problem. | How it works through Data, Tools, Practices, Actions; its bias, ethical boundaries, and impact |
| **II. Targeted AI Restriction** | Propose a limit, safeguard, or policy constraint on a system with documented or predictable harm. | The risky system; evidence-based rationale; DTPA analysis of why it's needed and how it would work |

**A domain is not a topic — for either pathway.** 

Narrow until you can name *who* would use the system (I) or *which* system, enforced by *whom* (II).

---

# What a Good Topic Looks Like

## Pathway I

- **Too Broad:** "Use AI to improve city services."
- **Reasonable Start:** *A model that helps a mid-sized Michigan city predict which homes have lead service lines, designed so residents can see and challenge their home's prediction.* (Builds from the Flint Water Crisis.)

## Pathway II

- **Too Broad:** "Regulate AI in insurance."
- **Reasonable Start:** *A requirement that any Medicare prior-authorization denial flagged by an algorithm be reviewed by a clinician in the same specialty before the patient is notified, with rates published quarterly.* (Builds on nH Predict.)

**The test:** could someone who disagrees with you argue against your proposal specifically? If the only objection is "AI is good/bad," it's still too general.
