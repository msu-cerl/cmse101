# What Should Be Different This Time

- Narrow from domain to case by **Wednesday**, not by the end of the week.
- Find sources about your **case**, not just your domain.
- Use more than one **kind** of source.

**Two things to push on this round:**

- **Source variety.** Law, Finance, and Hiring have strong *official records*: court filings, settlements, regulator reports (FTC, EEOC, SEC, CFPB, state AGs). News alone will be thin.
- **Who could change it.** In **Actions**, name who had the power to change/constrain the system (a regulator, a court, a union) and what they actually did.
