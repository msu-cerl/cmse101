# From Scaffold to Essay

The scaffolds asked you to cover **all four DTPA components** at about equal depth. The essay asks you to **decide what matters most**.

- **State a clear thesis.** One sentence: what you propose and why.
- **Name your crux:** the DTPA component(s) your design turns on, and argue for *why*.
- **Present the design precisely.** Specific enough to build (or enforce), and to argue against.
- **Take the strongest objection seriously**, not the easiest one.
- **Organize around your argument**, not the template headings.

*Most of what you wrote for Scaffolds 1 and 2 will still appear, but revised, reordered, and put to work.*
