Weekly Readings

Each week has a prep reading to complete before Monday’s class. Monday’s Concept session assumes you’ve done it — we unpack and argue with the reading rather than summarize it.

📌 The reading list is being built. See the schedule for the week-by-week structure and dates.

What each week’s reading page will include

  • The reading itself — with an honest time estimate
  • Why this reading — what it does for the week’s Concept and Case days
  • Prep questions — what to hold in mind while reading, not a comprehension quiz
  • Going further — optional sources for teams whose project touches this material

How readings connect to everything else

Reading feeds…How
Monday — ConceptThe mechanics you’ll unpack in class
Wednesday — CaseEvidence and vocabulary for the DTPA walkthrough
Exit ticketsDaily responses draw on the week’s reading
Weekly reflectionYour D2L post should engage the reading specifically
ProjectReadings double as source material for your team’s evidence base

📌 Accessibility: Readings are selected for availability and diverse perspectives, and provided in multiple formats. If you need an alternative format, contact your instructor — no explanation needed.
