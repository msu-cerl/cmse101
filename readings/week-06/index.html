Week 6: AI in Society#

Focus: Applications across domains

📚 Required Readings#

Primary Readings#

  1. “AI in Healthcare: Promises and Pitfalls” (25 min)

    • Diagnostic AI systems
    • Drug discovery and personalized medicine
    • Equity in healthcare AI applications

  2. “AI in Criminal Justice: Beyond Predictive Policing” (25 min)

    • Risk assessment algorithms in sentencing
    • Bias in criminal justice AI systems
    • Transparency and accountability challenges

Supplementary Resources#

  • “AI in Education: Opportunities and Risks” — Brief overview
  • Case studies from your field of interest

💭 Discussion Prompts#

  1. What are the unique ethical considerations in high-stakes domains like healthcare or criminal justice?
  2. How do we balance innovation benefits with preventing harm?
  3. Who should benefit most from AI applications?

📝 Preparation for Class#

  • Choose one domain where AI is being deployed (healthcare, education, transportation, finance, etc.)
  • Research: What does the AI system do? What problem does it solve?
  • Analyze: What are the potential benefits and harms for different groups?
