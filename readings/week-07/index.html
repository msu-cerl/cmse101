Week 7: Midterm Review & Synthesis#

Focus: Integration of Weeks 1-6 concepts

📚 Review Readings#

Key Concepts to Review#

  1. AI Fundamentals (Weeks 1-2)

    • What is AI/ML? How systems are trained.
    • Key terminology and basic processes

  2. Ethics and Governance (Weeks 3-4)

    • Bias and fairness definitions
    • Policy approaches and regulatory frameworks

  3. Societal Impact (Weeks 5-6)

    • Economic implications and employment
    • Domain-specific applications

Study Materials#

  • Glossary of key terms
  • Summary charts and timelines
  • Review questions from previous weeks

💭 Reflection Questions#

  1. How have your views on AI and society evolved over the past 6 weeks?
  2. What are the most important ethical challenges we face?
  3. How do technical, policy, and societal factors interconnect?

📝 Midterm Preparation#

  • Review all weekly reading discussion prompts
  • Revisit case studies from weeks 1-6
  • Prepare examples to illustrate key concepts
  • Formulate your own perspective on AI and society issues

🔗 Related Assessment#

See Midterm Assessment for details on the midterm exam format and expectations.

Office Hours: Schedule time with instructor to discuss any concepts you’d like to clarify before the midterm.
