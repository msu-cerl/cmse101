Week 8: Case Studies#

Focus: Deep dives into real-world examples

Supplementary Cases#

  • Facial recognition systems and civil liberties
  • Social media algorithms and misinformation
  • ChatGPT and generative AI capabilities/limitations
  • Select your own case study from current news

💭 Analysis Framework#

For each case study, consider:

  1. Technical: How does the AI system work?
  2. Social: Who is affected? How are different groups impacted differently?
  3. Ethical: What went wrong? What principles were violated?
  4. Policy: What regulatory or governance responses occurred?
  5. Lessons: What can we learn for future AI development?

📝 Preparation for Class#

  • Read assigned case studies thoroughly
  • Prepare a 3-minute case summary: system, context, issues, outcomes
  • Bring questions and observations to class discussion
