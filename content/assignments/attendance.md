Most of the real work in this course happens in the room with your group. Attendance is taken each day while you work on your case analyses, and it counts toward your Class Engagement grade.

**You get three (3) drops; no questions asked.**

Attendance and [exit tickets](/assignments/exit-tickets/) are linked: a ticket submitted on a day you weren't in class earns no credit. If something comes up and you miss a meeting, contact [Danny](mailto:caball14@msu.edu) within 24 hours — that day's exit ticket is typically excused unless missing class becomes a pattern.

See the [syllabus](/syllabus/#class-attendance-expected-engagement) for how attendance factors into your Class Engagement grade.
