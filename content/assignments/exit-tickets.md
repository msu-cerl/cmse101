# Exit Tickets

A short in-class response that closes each meeting. Credit-based, low-stakes, one for every class day (42 across the semester).

Exit tickets do double duty: they tell your instructor what landed and what didn't before the next class, and they give you a running record of your own thinking to draw on when you write your weekly [forum post](/assignments/forum-posts/).

## Format

- Short, in-class, low-stakes
- Credit-based (completion, not correctness)

## Rhythm

- Every class day
