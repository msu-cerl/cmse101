## Week 1 Assignment: AI Foundations Reflection

**Due:** End of Week 1 | **Format:** Discussion post or brief reflection | **Length:** 300-500 words

---

## 📝 Assignment Overview

This assignment asks you to reflect on your current understanding of AI and machine learning, and to start thinking critically about how these technologies appear in your life.

---

## 📋 Instructions

### Part 1: Reflection Essay (200-300 words)

Write a brief reflection addressing these prompts:

1. **Prior to this week**, what was your understanding of AI and machine learning? What misconceptions did you have?
2. **After the readings**, what surprised you most about how AI works?
3. **In your own life**, identify 2-3 examples of AI or ML you encounter (recommendation systems, voice assistants, search algorithms, etc.). How do they affect your daily experience?

### Part 2: Discussion Forum (100-200 words)

Post your response to the discussion forum and respond thoughtfully to at least two classmates' posts.

---

| Criteria | Points | Description |
|----------|--------|-------------|

## 💡 Tips for Success

- Use specific examples from your reading
- Be honest about what was confusing or challenging
- Engage with peers respectfully, even if you disagree
- Ask questions if you're unsure about concepts
