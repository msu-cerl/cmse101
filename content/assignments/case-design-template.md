# Case Design – Assignment Instructions  

**Course:** AI in the Real World: Data, Power, & Society  
**Students:** Teams of 3‑4  
**Framework:** Data‑Tools‑Practices‑Actions (DTPA)  

> **Important:** Review the official case‑design template before you begin (link in the “Resources” section).  

---  

## 1. Overview  

The Case Design project is the capstone of the semester. Your team will produce a **fully‑fledged case** that applies the DTPA framework to a real‑world AI issue. You may choose **one** of the two pathways:

| Pathway | Goal | What you must deliver |
|--------|------|------------------------|
| **I. Proactive AI Solution** | Design an AI‑driven intervention that tackles a societal problem. | • Description of the AI application  <br>• Explanation of how it works (Data, Tools, Practices, Actions) <br>• Analysis of bias, ethics, and impact |
| **II. Targeted AI Restriction** | Propose a safeguard, policy, or limitation on an existing AI system that poses risk. | • Identification of the risky AI system <br>• Rationale for the restriction (evidence‑based) <br>• DTPA analysis of why the restriction is necessary and how it would be implemented |

Both pathways require **evidence‑based arguments** and **APA‑style citations** drawn from peer‑reviewed literature, technical reports, news articles, economic data, or historical documentation.

## 2. Milestones & Scaffolded Assignments  

| Milestone | Deliverable | Word count (excl. references) | Due | What you’ll be assessed on |
|-----------|------------|------------------------------|-----|-----------------------------|
| **M1 – Scoping Brief** (Assignment 1) | • Problem statement or AI system description <br>• Preliminary research map (key sources, data types, stakeholders) <br>• Selected pathway (I or II) | 500 | **Week 4** (end of class) | Clarity of problem, relevance to DTPA, feasibility of scope, initial source quality |
| **M2 – DTPA Draft** (Assignment 2) | • Structured DTPA analysis (Data, Tools, Practices, Actions) <br>• Draft of the case narrative (no final conclusions) <br>• Annotated bibliography (APA) | 1 000 | **Week 8** (mid‑semester) | Depth of DTPA application, quality of evidence, logical flow, correct APA citations |
| **M3 – Full Case Design** (Final Project) | • Complete case (final narrative, recommendations/policy, implementation plan) <br>• Full APA reference list <br>• **Response Document** (see below) | 2 000 | **Week 14** (final week) | Integration of all DTPA components, originality, rigor of evidence, feasibility of solution or restriction, writing quality |
| **M4 – Feedback Response** (Post‑final) | • Separate 500‑word document addressing each instructor comment point‑by‑point, describing revisions made or why a suggestion was not adopted. | 500 | **Week 15** (final submission deadline) | Ability to incorporate feedback, reflective thinking, clarity of response |

> **All assignments are submitted through D2L**. Late work is accepted only with documented extenuating circumstances (see syllabus).

## 3. Detailed Scaffold Instructions  

### **Assignment 1 – Scoping Brief (500 words)**  

1. **Problem or System Description** – Explain the societal issue (Path I) or the AI system you aim to restrict (Path II).  
2. **Why it matters** – Briefly cite at least two sources showing the significance of the problem or risk.  
3. **Preliminary DTPA Lens** – Outline one bullet for each DTPA component that you expect to explore.  
4. **Research Map** – List 4–6 key sources you will use (journal article, report, news story, data set, etc.). Include a placeholder APA citation for each.  
5. **Team Roles** – Assign a short responsibility (e.g., Data lead, Tools lead, Practices lead, Actions lead, Coordination).  

**Evaluation:** Scope clarity, relevance to course goals, evidence of research planning, and equitable division of labor.  

---  

### **Assignment 2 – DTPA Draft (1 000 words)**  

1. **Data** – Describe the data set(s) you will rely on, collection methods, inclusion/exclusion criteria, and potential biases.  
2. **Tools** – Summarize the algorithmic or computational tool(s) (e.g., classifier, LLM, image‑recognition model). Discuss underlying assumptions and technical limits.  
3. **Practices** – Analyze who creates, curates, and deploys the tool; labor considerations; regulatory or institutional contexts.  
4. **Actions** – Explain the intended outcomes, who benefits, who may be harmed, and what incentives the AI system creates.  
5. **Draft Narrative** – Combine the four sections into a cohesive case description (≈ 800 words).  
6. **Annotated Bibliography** – Provide full APA citations with a 2‑sentence annotation for each source explaining relevance.  

**Evaluation:** Depth of DTPA analysis, quality and relevance of sources, logical coherence, correct APA formatting.  

---  

### **Final Case Design (2 000 words)**  

Your final deliverable expands the DTPA Draft into a polished, actionable case:  

| Section | Content |
|---------|---------|
| **Executive Summary** (≈ 150 words) | One‑paragraph overview of the problem, your proposed solution or restriction, and expected impact. |
| **Full DTPA Analysis** | Extend each component with concrete evidence, quantitative indicators (where possible), and direct quotes from sources. |
| **Implementation / Policy Plan** | For Path I: rollout steps, stakeholder engagement, monitoring metrics.<br>For Path II: enforcement mechanism, compliance checks, mitigation strategies. |
| **Risk & Ethical Assessment** | Identify remaining uncertainties, potential unintended consequences, and mitigation measures. |
| **Conclusion** | Synthesize findings and state why your design meets the course’s “critical pragmatism” standard. |
| **References** | Complete APA list (no annotations needed). |

**Evaluation:** Comprehensive integration of DTPA, rigor of evidence, feasibility, originality, and professional writing.  

---  

### **Feedback Response Document (500 words)**  

After you receive the instructor’s rubric and comments on the final case:  

1. **Quote each comment** (or reference the comment number).  
2. **State the change you made** (or explain why you chose not to change it).  
3. **Locate the revision** (e.g., “Section 3, paragraph 2”).  

This document demonstrates reflective practice and shows you can iterate based on scholarly critique.

## 4. Grading Rubric (High‑Level)  

| Category | Weight | Key Indicators |
|----------|--------|----------------|
| **Scoping Brief** | 10 % | Clear problem definition, feasible scope, solid initial sources, balanced team roles |
| **DTPA Draft** | 20 % | Depth of each DTPA component, sound evidence, APA accuracy, coherent draft |
| **Final Case Design** | 55 % | Full DTPA integration, evidence quality, realistic implementation, ethical rigor, writing quality |
| **Feedback Response** | 10 % | Thoroughness of response, evidence of revision, reflective insight |
| **Collaboration** (peer‑assessment) | 5 % | Equal participation, clear communication, conflict resolution |
