* **Students:** Teams of 3-4
* **Framework:** Data-Tools-Practices-Actions (DTPA)

The Case Design project is the principal assessment for the course. Your team develops one case, in stages, over the whole semester. Your chosen topic must be substantial enough in scope to warrant a group effort.

Unlike Case Studies, this is not three separate documents. It is **one document that grows**: your Final Case Design is a direct expansion of your Scaffold 2, which is a direct expansion of your Scaffold 1. Each scaffold gets instructor feedback, and the next scaffold must respond to that feedback in a separate document.

## Choose a Pathway

| Pathway | Goal | What you must deliver |
|--------|------|------------------------|
| **I. Proactive AI Solution** | Design an AI application that directly addresses a societal problem. | Description of the application; explanation of how it works through Data, Tools, Practices, and Actions; analysis of its bias, ethical boundaries, and impact |
| **II. Targeted AI Restriction** | Propose a limit, safeguard, or policy constraint on an existing AI system whose deployment poses documented or predictable harm. | Identification of the risky system; evidence-based rationale for the restriction; DTPA analysis of why it's needed and how it would be implemented |

Both pathways require evidence-based arguments and APA-style citations: peer-reviewed journals, scientific reports, news coverage, empirical studies, economic projections, or historical documentation. See the [APA formatting guide](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/references/examples).

# How to Use the Template

Copy the whole template below into one shared document for your team. Complete **Part 1** for Scaffold 1. When Scaffold 2 is due, don't delete Part 1, expand it into **Part 2**, and address your instructor's feedback in a separate response document. Do the same again for **Part 3** at the final deadline. By the end, your one document tells the whole story of your case, start to finish.

## Case Design Template

* **Team members:**
* **Pathway chosen (I or II):**
*  **Working title / topic:**

### Part 1: Scoping (Scaffold 1) - Due Oct 2

* **Problem or system description.** The societal issue (Pathway I) or the AI system you aim to restrict (Pathway II).
* **Why it matters.** At least two cited sources showing the significance of the problem or risk.
* **Preliminary DTPA lens.** One paragraph per component you expect to explore: Data, Tools, Practices, Actions.
* **Research map.** 4-6 sources you plan to use, with placeholder APA citations.
* **Team roles.** A lead for each DTPA component, plus coordination.

**While you are free to design the format of Part 1, it should be considered a formal report (check spelling and grammar; ensure the document flows well).**

### Part 2: DTPA Analysis (Scaffold 2, expands Part 1) - Due Nov 6

The level of detail for Part 2 should be increased in both depth and sophistication as you have studied more examples in class. Remember that you should dive into the details of design case from the DTPA framework. Here, the [template for case analysis](case-studies) might help you in developing the depth necesssary.

* **Data.** The dataset(s) involved, collection methods, inclusion and exclusion criteria, potential biases.
* **Tools.** The algorithm or model involved (classifier, LLM, neural net, etc.), its assumptions, and its technical limits.
* **Practices.** Who creates, curates, and deploys the tool; labor considerations; regulatory or institutional context.
* **Actions.** Intended outcomes, who benefits, who may be harmed, what incentives the system creates.
* **Annotated bibliography.** Full APA citations, with a two-sentence annotation per source explaining its relevance.

**While you are free to design the format of Part 2, it should be considered a formal report (check spelling and grammar; ensure the document flows well).**

*(Attach separately: response to Scaffold 1 feedback.)*

### Part 3: Full Case Design (Final, expands Part 2) - Due Dec 4th

Your final case design should provide a complete answer to either pathway you have selected and include a summary of the solution or restriction, a detailed analysis using DTPA, an implementation or polciy plan, and a risk/ethics assessment. Your work should be grounded in evidence and have clear references to the work informing your case design.

* **Executive summary** (about 150 words). The problem, your proposed solution or restriction, and expected impact.
* **Full DTPA analysis.** Each component from Part 2, deepened with concrete evidence and direct citations.
* **Implementation or policy plan.** Pathway I: rollout steps, stakeholder engagement, monitoring metrics. Pathway II: enforcement mechanism, compliance checks, mitigation strategies.
* **Risk and ethical assessment.** Remaining uncertainties, unintended consequences, mitigation measures.
* **Conclusion.** Why the design reflects a critically pragmatic view of AI.
* **References.** Full APA list.

*(Attach separately: response to Scaffold 2 feedback.)*

**While you are free to design the format of Part 3, it should be considered a formal report (check spelling and grammar; ensure the document flows well).**

### Feedback Response Document (attach at Scaffold 2 and Final) 

For each piece of instructor feedback:

1. Quote or reference the comment.
2. State the change you made, or explain why you did not make it.
3. Point to where the revision appears (for example, "Part 2, Data section").


### Class Showcase - Occurring Dec 7th-11th

To ensure each member of our class has the opportunity to see your work and provide feedback, the last week of classes will consist of in-class presentations of your case design project. Here, we expect you to lead the class through your complete case design analysis in roughly 10 minutes. Presentation slides are highly recommended.

While itself not a graded assignment, you will not be able to turn in your final case design without presenting your case and considering the class feedback and questions. This feedback should inform the final case design that is turned in Dec 15th.
