## Week 5 Assignment: AI & Employment Analysis

**Due:** End of Week 5 | **Format:** Research report | **Length:** 700-900 words

---

## 📝 Assignment Overview

Investigate how AI and automation might impact a specific occupation, industry, or sector you care about. Develop an informed assessment of risks and opportunities, along with policy recommendations.

---

## 📋 Instructions

### Choose Your Focus

Select an occupation or industry you're interested in:
- Examples: healthcare, education, manufacturing, agriculture, retail, finance, law, transportation, construction, etc.

### Research & Analysis (700-900 words)

**1. Current State (100-150 words)**
- Describe the occupation/industry
- What are typical tasks and skills?
- How is AI already being used (if at all)?

**2. Potential AI Impact (150-200 words)**
- What tasks could be automated or significantly changed by AI?
- What's the timeline: near-term (1-3 years) vs. long-term (5-10 years)?
- How many workers might be affected? In what ways?
- Which workers are most vulnerable?

**3. Opportunities & Benefits (100-150 words)**
- How could AI improve working conditions?
- What new types of jobs might emerge?
- How could AI augment human workers rather than replace them?
- What workers might benefit most?

**4. Challenges & Risks (100-150 words)**
- Job displacement and income loss
- Widening inequality if benefits aren't shared
- Skills gaps and retraining needs
- Psychological and social impacts

**5. Policy Recommendations (200-250 words)**
- What policies should governments pursue?
- What responsibility do companies have?
- How should workers be supported?
- Options to consider:
  - Retraining and education programs
  - Wage subsidies or income support
  - Regulatory guardrails on AI deployment
  - Worker representation in decision-making
  - Profit-sharing or taxation approaches

## 💡 Tips for Success

- Combine research with your own experience or interviews
- Use data when available, but also qualitative insights
- Avoid both tech-utopian and catastrophic predictions
- Ground recommendations in feasible policies
- Cite academic research, policy reports, and news sources
