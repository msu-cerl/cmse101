# Lesson Planning & Leading Class

After the first few weeks of class, groups of students (3–4) can opt in to plan and lead a lesson. Expect this to take each student roughly 10 hours to plan and lead. This is a **deeper engagement** option, not a requirement.

## How to sign up

- Reach out to your [instructor](mailto:caball14@msu.edu) at least one week before your planned lesson
- A student may only participate once
- Scheduling depends on availability — your lesson may land later in the semester than requested

## What's expected

- Learning goals for the session
- Engagement strategies
- An appropriate Exit Ticket for your lesson

📌 See the [syllabus](/syllabus/#class-engagement) for how leading a class factors into your Class Engagement grade.
