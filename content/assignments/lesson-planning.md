Starting in Week 11, groups can opt in to lead **half of a Monday or Wednesday class** by walking the room through one of your group's three Full Case studies (I, II, or III) using the DTPA framework. This is a **deeper engagement** option, not a requirement. A student may only participate once.

The other half of that class period, and all of Friday, is Case Design work time. If no group has signed up for a given half-class, that time is Case Design work too.

## What's expected

Pick **one** of your group's three Full Case analyses. Walk the class through it using the DTPA framework, highlighting your strongest evidence and any disagreements your group had along the way, then pose at least one discussion question to the room. This is deliberately lighter than a full lesson plan: you already did the research for this case, so the job here is presenting and facilitating discussion on it, not designing new content.

Your group also writes that day's **Exit Ticket** question, tied to your case. It still runs at the normal end of class, same as any other day.

📌 See the [syllabus](/syllabus/#class-engagement) for how leading a class factors into your Class Engagement grade.

## Session Plan Template

Send this to me at least three days before your slot.

* **Team members:**
* **Date / slot:**
* **Which Full Case (I, II, or III), and topic:**

### 1. What you'll present

* Brief summary of the case and why it's a useful example.
* Your strongest piece of evidence for one or two DTPA components (don't try to re-present the whole analysis in half a class).
* Any disagreements your group had, and how you resolved (or didn't resolve) them.

### 2. Discussion question(s)

* What will you ask the room to get people talking?
* What's your follow-up if the room goes quiet?

### 3. Exit Ticket

* What question will you ask to check whether the class engaged with your case?

---

### Post-Session Reflection (individual, after you lead)

**Required to receive credit (~150 words); due Sunday at 11:59pm instead of your case analysis that week**

* What went well?
* What would you change if you did this again?
* What surprised you about how the class engaged with your case?
