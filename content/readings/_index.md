# Weekly Readings

Each week has a **prep reading** to complete before Monday's class. Monday's Concept session assumes you've done it — we unpack and argue with the reading rather than summarize it.

> 📌 The reading list is being built. See the [schedule](/schedule/) for the week-by-week structure and dates.

---

## What each week's reading page will include

- **The reading itself** — with an honest time estimate
- **Why this reading** — what it does for the week's Concept and Case days
- **Prep questions** — what to hold in mind while reading, not a comprehension quiz
- **Going further** — optional sources for teams whose project touches this material

---

## How readings connect to everything else

| Reading feeds… | How |
|----------------|-----|
| **Monday — Concept** | The mechanics you'll unpack in class |
| **Wednesday — Case** | Evidence and vocabulary for the [DTPA](/learning-goals/) walkthrough |
| **Exit tickets** | Daily responses draw on the week's reading |
| **Weekly reflection** | Your D2L post should engage the reading specifically |
| **Project** | Readings double as source material for your team's evidence base |

---

📌 **Accessibility:** Readings are selected for availability and diverse perspectives, and provided in multiple formats. If you need an alternative format, contact your [instructor](mailto:caball14@msu.edu) — no explanation needed.
