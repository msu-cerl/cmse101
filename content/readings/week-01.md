## Week 1: Fundamentals of AI

**Focus:** What is AI/ML? Brief history and overview

---

## 📚 Required Readings

### Primary Readings

1. **"Artificial Intelligence: A Brief History"** (15 min read)
   - Overview of AI development from symbolic AI to modern machine learning
   - Key figures and pivotal moments in AI research

2. **"What is Machine Learning?"** (20 min read)
   - Difference between AI and machine learning
   - Supervised vs. unsupervised learning basics
   - Real-world examples of ML applications

### Supplementary Resources

- Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: Artificial Intelligence ([link](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/artificial-intelligence/)) — Philosophical foundations
- "A Very Short Introduction to Artificial Intelligence" — Excerpts (10 min)

## 💭 Discussion Prompts

Before class, consider these questions as you read:

1. How do you currently encounter AI in your daily life?
2. What misconceptions about AI do these readings challenge?
3. What questions remain unanswered about AI fundamentals?
