## 📚 Curated Resources

### Key Organizations & Research Centers

- [AI Now Institute](https://ainowinstitute.org/) — Research on AI's societal impacts
- [Partnership on AI](https://partnershiponai.org/) — Collaborative platform for responsible AI
- [Center for Security and Emerging Technology (CSET)](https://cset.georgetown.edu/) — AI policy and safety
- [Stanford Internet Observatory](https://cyber.stanford.edu/) — Tech policy research
- [Data & Society](https://datasociety.net/) — Social implications of technology
