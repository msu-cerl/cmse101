## 📚 Curated Resources

### Key Organizations & Research Centers

- [AI Now Institute](https://ainowinstitute.org/) — Research on AI's societal impacts
- [Partnership on AI](https://partnershiponai.org/) — Collaborative platform for responsible AI
- [Center for Security and Emerging Technology (CSET)](https://cset.georgetown.edu/) — AI policy and safety
- [Stanford Internet Observatory](https://cyber.stanford.edu/) — Tech policy research
- [Data & Society](https://datasociety.net/) — Social implications of technology
- [Electronic Frontier Foundation (EFF)](https://www.eff.org/) — Digital rights nonprofit; privacy, free expression, and surveillance litigation

### Books

- [*Weapons of Math Destruction*](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/241363/weapons-of-math-destruction-by-cathy-oneil/) — Cathy O'Neil, on how scoring algorithms in finance, policing, and education can quietly encode and amplify inequity
- [*Automating Inequality*](https://us.macmillan.com/books/9781250215789/automatinginequality/) — Virginia Eubanks, on algorithmic decision-making in the American welfare system
- [*Race After Technology*](https://www.politybooks.com/bookdetail?book_slug=race-after-technology--9781509526406) — Ruha Benjamin, on how bias gets built into tools that look neutral on the surface
- [*Algorithms of Oppression*](https://nyupress.org/9781479837243/algorithms-of-oppression/) — Safiya Umoja Noble, on how search engines reproduce racism and sexism
- [*Atlas of AI*](https://yalebooks.yale.edu/book/9780300264630/atlas-of-ai/) — Kate Crawford, on the labor, data, and natural resources an AI system actually costs
- [*Artificial Unintelligence: How Computers Misunderstand the World*](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262537018/artificial-unintelligence/) — Meredith Broussard, a data journalist's case against "technochauvinism"
- [*Artificial Intelligence: A Guide for Thinking Humans*](https://us.macmillan.com/books/9781250758040/artificialintelligence/) — Melanie Mitchell, the clearest plain-language account of how these systems actually work (and don't)
- [*The Age of Surveillance Capitalism*](https://www.hachettebookgroup.com/titles/shoshana-zuboff/the-age-of-surveillance-capitalism/9781610395694/) — Shoshana Zuboff, on data extraction as a business model
- [*The Ethics of Artificial Intelligence: Principles, Challenges, and Opportunities*](https://www.barnesandnoble.com/w/the-ethics-of-artificial-intelligence-luciano-floridi/1143428330) — Luciano Floridi (Oxford University Press), a short, structured overview of the ethical questions AI raises
- [*The Internet Con: How to Seize the Means of Computation*](https://www.versobooks.com/products/3035-the-internet-con) — Cory Doctorow, on monopoly power, platforms, and who actually controls the infrastructure AI runs on
- [*The Reverse Centaur's Guide to Life After AI*](https://www.versobooks.com/products/3584-the-reverse-centaur-s-guide-to-life-after-ai) - Cory Doctorow, on what happens next with AI and what we can do about it
- [*Teaching Machines: The History of Personalized Learning*](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262045698/teaching-machines/) — Audrey Watters, a history of ed-tech's decades-old promises, well before Silicon Valley showed up

### Podcasts

- [*Your Undivided Attention*](https://www.humanetech.com/podcast) — Center for Humane Technology; how technology is designed to capture attention, and what that design costs us
- [*Tech Won't Save Us*](https://www.techwontsave.us/) — Paris Marx; a skeptical, historically grounded look at tech-industry narratives
- [*This Machine Kills*](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/this-machine-kills/id1526914048) — Jathan Sadowski and Edward Ongweso Jr.; the political economy of technology
- [*In Machines We Trust*](https://www.technologyreview.com/supertopic/in-machines-we-trust/) — MIT Technology Review, hosted by Jennifer Strong; AI's everyday, on-the-ground impact
- [*Better Offline*](https://www.betteroffline.com/) — Ed Zitron; a critical read on the AI industry and its hype cycle
- [*Kill Switch*](https://www.iheart.com/podcast/105-kill-switch-30880104/) — iHeartPodcasts/Kaleidoscope, hosted by Dexter Thomas; a plain-language guide to the tech and algorithms running our lives
- [*The 404 Media Podcast*](https://www.404media.co/the-404-media-podcast/) — the reporters behind 404 Media on the stories they're breaking that week
