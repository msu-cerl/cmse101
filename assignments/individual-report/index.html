Individual Effort & Metacognitive Report

A short individual write-up (~250 words) that accompanies each week’s case study. Everyone in a group writes their own, and they are not shared with your groupmates.

Individual Effort & Metacognitive Reports are turned in on D2L as an individual upload, due 11:59pm the Sunday following that week’s classes.

Cover three things:

  • Effort — what did you actually contribute to the group’s case analysis?
  • Learning — how is your learning going on this week’s subject matter? What went well, what needs to improve?
  • Values — how do your own values and views shape the way you read this case?

You also need to disclose any AI use in your work here.

I read these alongside the other reports from your group. They’re how I check that everyone is meeting a minimum standard of engagement, and how I notice when someone is going well beyond it.

You get one (1) drop; no questions asked.

See the syllabus for how this factors into your grade.
