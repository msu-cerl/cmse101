Week 9 Assignment: Privacy & Emerging Tech Analysis#

Due: End of Week 9 | Format: Research analysis | Length: 900-1100 words

📝 Assignment Overview#

Investigate an emerging AI technology or practice that raises privacy and surveillance concerns. Analyze current impacts, implications, and policy responses.

📋 Topic Options#

Choose one focus area:

Privacy & Data Collection:

  • Facial recognition systems and privacy
  • Biometric data collection and use
  • Location tracking and surveillance
  • Data brokers and data markets

Surveillance Systems:

  • Mass surveillance infrastructure
  • Predictive policing and monitoring
  • Workplace monitoring and surveillance
  • Social credit systems

Generative AI Issues:

  • Copyright and training data ethics
  • Environmental costs of large AI models
  • Misinformation and deepfakes
  • Data privacy in language models

Other Emerging Tech:

  • Genetic databases and discrimination risk
  • Commercial vs. government surveillance
  • Your choice (get approval)

📋 Analysis Structure (900-1100 words)#

1. Technology Description (150 words)

  • What is the technology?
  • How does it work?
  • Who is deploying it? Where?
  • Scale and growth trajectory

2. Current Uses & Adoption (150 words)

  • Current applications and users
  • Benefits claimed by proponents
  • Growth or expansion plans
  • Who profits or gains power?

3. Privacy & Surveillance Concerns (250 words)

  • What specific risks to privacy/surveillance?
  • What data is collected/used?
  • How could data be misused?
  • What happens if data is breached?
  • Impacts on marginalized communities?

4. Existing Safeguards & Regulations (150 words)

  • What laws/regulations apply currently?
  • Technical safeguards in place?
  • Are they adequate?
  • What gaps exist?

5. Policy Recommendations (250 words)

  • What needs to change?
  • How should this technology be governed?
  • Balance between innovation and protection
  • International considerations
  • Options:
    • Stronger regulations
    • Technical standards
    • Transparency requirements
    • Individual rights (right to opt-out, access, deletion, etc.)
    • Oversight mechanisms

6. Broader Questions (100 words)

  • What does this reveal about power and technology?
  • How does this connect to other AI concerns?
  • What’s at stake for society?

📌 Rubric#

CriteriaPointsDescription
Understanding20%Accurate, detailed explanation of technology and issues
Research20%Well-sourced; uses current reporting and analysis
Privacy Analysis25%Thoughtful examination of surveillance/privacy concerns
Policy Reasoning20%Realistic recommendations grounded in evidence
Communication15%Clear, engaging, well-organized writing

💡 Tips for Success#

  • Go beyond surface-level description
  • Find real examples and case studies
  • Consider impacts on different groups
  • Acknowledge tradeoffs and complexity
  • Use recent sources (tech changes rapidly)
  • Connect to broader course themes
