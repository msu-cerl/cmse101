Midterm Assessment#

Date: End of Week 7 | Format: Exam or project | Duration: 2-3 hours (exam) or equivalent effort (project)

📝 Assessment Overview#

The midterm assesses your understanding of core concepts from Weeks 1-6 and your ability to analyze and apply them to new situations.

📋 Format Options#

Option A: Traditional Exam#

Structure:

  • Part 1: Multiple Choice (20 questions, 30 min)

    • Concepts, key figures, historical facts, definitions

  • Part 2: Short Answer (3 questions, 45 min)

    • Explain key concepts (e.g., “What is algorithmic bias?”)
    • Connect ideas (e.g., “How do training data decisions affect AI outcomes?”)

  • Part 3: Essay (1 question, 60 min)

    • Analyze a case study or scenario
    • Apply course concepts to new situation

Option B: Comprehensive Project#

Choose one:

  1. Case Study Deep Dive

    • Analyze a bias case from the course in greater depth
    • Develop actionable recommendations

  2. Policy Synthesis

    • Compare 2-3 AI governance approaches
    • Argue for a comprehensive framework

  3. Multimedia Presentation

    • Create a 15-20 minute presentation or video
    • Explain one major theme from the first half of the course

📌 Content Covered#

Conceptual Knowledge:

  • AI and machine learning fundamentals
  • How AI systems are trained and tested
  • Types and sources of algorithmic bias
  • Policy and governance frameworks
  • Economic impacts and employment concerns
  • Domain-specific applications and implications

Skills:

  • Analyzing complex, ambiguous situations
  • Identifying stakeholders and their interests
  • Applying ethical frameworks to AI systems
  • Evaluating policy tradeoffs
  • Communicating nuanced perspectives

📋 Study Tips#

  1. Review weekly materials: Readings, discussions, assignments
  2. Make concept map: Connect key ideas together
  3. Study case studies: Be able to explain what went wrong and why
  4. Prepare examples: Have 3-4 concrete examples ready to discuss
  5. Practice explaining: Can you explain concepts to someone unfamiliar?
  6. Review feedback: Look at your assignment feedback from Weeks 1-6

📌 Exam Rubric (if traditional exam)#

CriteriaPointsDescription
Knowledge25%Accurate recall and understanding of concepts
Application40%Apply concepts to new scenarios; analyze situations
Critical Thinking20%Identify tradeoffs, acknowledge complexity
Communication15%Clear, organized responses; proper terminology

💡 Exam Day Tips#

  • Arrive early
  • Bring ID and materials as specified
  • Read questions carefully
  • Budget your time (don’t spend 40 min on one question)
  • Sketch an outline before writing essays
  • Show your reasoning, even if unsure

📌 Accommodations#

Registered with ODS? Ensure your accommodations letter is submitted to your instructor at least 1 week before the exam.
