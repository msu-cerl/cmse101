Week 6 Assignment: Domain-Specific AI Application Analysis#

Due: End of Week 6 | Format: Analytical report with multimedia | Length: 800-1000 words + visuals

📝 Assignment Overview#

Choose a domain where AI is being deployed (healthcare, criminal justice, education, finance, etc.) and conduct a comprehensive analysis of benefits, risks, and ethical implications for different stakeholders.

📋 Instructions#

Domain Selection#

Choose one domain:

  • Healthcare: Diagnostic AI, drug discovery, personalized medicine, robotic surgery
  • Criminal Justice: Risk assessment, policing, sentencing, parole decisions
  • Education: Personalized learning, essay grading, student performance prediction
  • Finance: Loan decisions, fraud detection, algorithmic trading
  • Employment: Hiring, performance evaluation, scheduling
  • Transportation: Autonomous vehicles, traffic management, logistics
  • Your choice (get instructor approval)

Analysis (800-1000 words)#

1. The Application (150 words)

  • What specific AI application(s) in this domain?
  • What problem does it solve?
  • How does it work (in accessible terms)?
  • Who benefits from its use?

2. Stakeholders & Impacts (200 words) Analyze impacts on different groups:

  • Users/patients/clients: What’s the benefit? What are the risks?
  • Workers: Is automation displacing jobs? Augmenting work?
  • Providers/organizations: What are incentives to deploy AI?
  • Society: What broader implications?
  • Consider how impacts vary across demographics (class, race, gender, geography, etc.)

3. Ethical Issues (200 words)

  • What ethical principles are relevant? (autonomy, fairness, transparency, accountability, etc.)
  • What tensions or tradeoffs exist?
  • What could go wrong?
  • Who is most vulnerable to harm?

4. Regulatory & Governance (200 words)

  • What regulations currently apply?
  • Are they adequate? What gaps exist?
  • What accountability mechanisms exist?
  • What more needs to be done?

5. Your Informed Perspective (250 words)

  • Based on your analysis, should this AI be deployed more widely, paused, or heavily regulated?
  • What conditions would make it more acceptable?
  • What’s your reasoning?
  • What questions remain?

📌 Required Elements#

  • Main Report: 800-1000 words
  • Visuals: At least 1-2 charts, diagrams, or infographics
  • Case Study: Include 1-2 real-world examples (successes or failures)
  • References: At least 8-10 sources (mix of academic, policy, and journalism)

📌 Rubric#

CriteriaPointsDescription
Understanding20%Accurate technical and contextual understanding
Stakeholder Analysis25%Thorough analysis of different impacts and perspectives
Ethical Reasoning25%Sophisticated engagement with ethical issues
Evidence & Examples15%Well-researched, concrete examples
Presentation15%Clear writing, effective visuals, proper citations

💡 Tips for Success#

  • Interview or survey people in this domain if possible
  • Use peer-reviewed research alongside current reporting
  • Visualize complex tradeoffs
  • Avoid jargon; explain technical concepts clearly
  • Present multiple perspectives fairly before offering your own
